Questions

Things teams ask about this service.

How do you stop AI assistants from giving wrong answers?

We connect assistants to curated, continuously updated sources using retrieval (RAG), set confidence controls, and route sensitive or high-risk questions to a person.

Will an agent replace my team?

No. Agents take the repetitive first steps. People stay in charge of relationships, exceptions and decisions.
