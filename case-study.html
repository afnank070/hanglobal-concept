The challenge

Leads arrived at all hours. Replies did not.

Enquiries came in from property portals, the website and Instagram, day and night. Agents replied when they could, often hours later, and follow-up lived in personal inboxes. Managers had no shared view of the pipeline, and good leads went cold.

Before
4 h 20 min
After
Under 1 min

Our approach

Pilot one office, then scale.

  1. Weeks 1 to 2

    Discover

    Mapped how leads arrive, who replies, and where information gets lost. Agreed three KPIs: first reply time, qualified leads and viewings booked.

  2. Weeks 3 to 5

    Design and pilot

    Built and tested the agent with one office, with agents reviewing every handover.

  3. Weeks 6 to 7

    Integrate

    Connected the agent to HubSpot and each agent's calendar, so records and bookings happen on their own.

  4. Week 8

    Launch and train

    Rolled out to all six offices with hands-on training and a weekly results review.

The solution

An agent that replies, qualifies and books.

The agent answers every enquiry on the website, Instagram and email within seconds, asks the right questions, and hands a complete summary to the right agent.

  • Instant first reply on every channel, any hour
  • Qualification on budget, timeline and area
  • Viewings booked straight into agent calendars
  • Every conversation and field written to HubSpot
  • A person takes over whenever the lead asks for one

The outcome

What changed in the first quarter.

35%more qualified leads reaching agents
22 hof admin saved per week, per office
3xmore viewings booked outside office hours
Every enquiry now gets an answer while it is still warm, and our agents walk into viewings knowing the whole story.Head of Sales, Northbridge Realty
